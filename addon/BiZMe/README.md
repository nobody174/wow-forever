# BiZMe!

Plan your gear: **BiZ** (best in slot) for **Me**.

BiZMe is a WoW Forever addon (part of the Road to Forever family) that:
- detects your class and spec from your talents
- shows BiZ, Pre-BiZ and per-phase gear lists, and best available gear at your level
- lets you browse any class/spec to plan alts or help friends (and share builds over Discord)
- filters by source: dungeon, raid, quest, reputation, PvP, crafted, world
- ranks the next best upgrade you can get now and, later, routes you to it

Builds on the existing Mr.BiZ (R2F BiS tab and site BiS page) and its ForeverDB-based data. Status: planning / research (Phase 0). See ROADMAP.md and BACKLOG.md.

Folders: `addon/BiZMe` (code, UI) and `addon/BiZMe_Data` (generated data, load-on-demand).
