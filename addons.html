Our own addon

Road to Forever v0.66.3 Download
Latest version 0.66.3 · Oct 16, 2026 · pre-release
Details
Made by us, free, and it never touches the internet: everything moves between this site and the game by copy and paste. Type /r2f or click its minimap button. What is in it:
  • Macros: every macro from our Macros page is built in; drag them from the Macro Book onto your bars. Pick your own set on the site and paste one string to import it.
  • Talents: a sandbox calculator for any class, recommended builds with a "next talent point" hint, saved loadouts, sharing with teammates, and Learn this build that spends the points for you (your own class, after a trainer reset, never in combat).
  • Launch plan: the checklist from our Launch Plan in game, with a waypoint arrow (eight looks) and a corpse arrow.
  • Mr.BiZ: best-in-slot gear for every class and spec with where it drops, plus a wishlist and the enchants.
  • Training and Home: what to train after a level-up, your next talent point, and a notice when a newer version is out.
  • Reminders and counters: Warrior stance, Hunter ammo, repair, pet, rested XP, food buff, Paladin seal and Rogue Slice and Dice timers, missing tracking and group buffs.
  • Tweaks (all off until you tick them): auto-repair and selling greys, quest auto-accept and turn-in, mail to your other characters, bag sorting, a copy-chat box and more.
  • Professions: exports your recipes to our Crafters page.
Install: download RoadToForever-0.66.3.zip from the release page and unzip it into Interface/AddOns/, so you end up with Interface/AddOns/RoadToForever/RoadToForever.toc.

ThreatMaster Download
Forever version 0.4.0 · Sep 28, 2026
Details
A threat meter styled after the game's built-in Damage Meter. Shows threat standing against the current target and warns both DPS and tanks when aggro is about to switch. Slash command conflict: claims /tm for itself, which is also the shorthand for WoW's built-in /targetmarker command — if you have a raid-mark macro using /tm (like our own mark macros used to), it'll silently call ThreatMaster instead of marking your target. Fixed on our macros page by switching to the full /targetmarker name — do the same for any of your own macros using /tm.
