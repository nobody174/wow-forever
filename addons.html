Our own addon

Road to Forever Download
Latest version 0.23.0 · Oct 9, 2026 · pre-release
Details
Made by us. Pick macros on our Macros page, paste one string in game, and drag them onto your bars from its Macro Book. It also imports talent builds from our Talent Calc links. It also exports your professions for the Crafters page. Type /r2f or click its minimap button.
Test first: since 0.11.0 it learns talents for you with one click, but that has never run on a live WoW Forever server. Try it with one talent point on a throwaway character before you use it on a main.
Install: download RoadToForever-0.23.0.zip from the release page and unzip it into Interface/AddOns/, so you end up with Interface/AddOns/RoadToForever/RoadToForever.toc.

What we use

Archivist for Forever Download
Forever version 1.0.7 · Oct 4, 2026
Details
Library book tracker built into the Professions window: which books you've turned in, which are in your bags or bank, where the missing ones are, and the rewards. Pairs well with our Library Books route. Use this Forever version; the older "Archivist" on CurseForge is an unrelated Retail addon.
AtlasLoot Continued Download
Forever version 12482 · Oct 5, 2026
Details
In-game loot tables for every boss, dungeon and raid. One download covers Classic, TBC and WoW Forever; the CurseForge app picks the right files for you.
Forever Bestiary Download
Forever version 0.5.0 · Sep 30, 2026
Details
Hunter pet bestiary: every tameable beast with its 3D model, family and abilities, so you can plan which pet to tame before you head out.
ForeverAuras Download
Forever version 0.50.6-BETA.1 · Oct 5, 2026
Details
A WeakAuras fork made for WoW Forever, replacing WeakAuras Forever (pulled from CurseForge on Oct 5). Shows custom icons, bars and alerts for buffs, debuffs and cooldowns. Open it with /fa or its minimap icon. Still in active development during the beta, so expect frequent updates.
Recommended aura: Battle Shout reminder (Warrior). ForeverAuras is built from WeakAuras, so WeakAuras import strings should work, but we haven't tried this one in it yet.
  1. Open the link and click "Copy Classic-WeakAura import string".
  2. In game, type /fa and click Import.
  3. Paste and click Done.
ForeverPlus Download
Forever version 0.8.6 · Oct 5, 2026
Details
Modular quality-of-life addon — quest automation, auto-sell/repair at merchants, buff tracking, an info bar, and a customizable tooltip system. Everything toggles individually and profiles can be shared between characters.
Leatrix Maps Download
Forever version 1.60.12-forever · Oct 5, 2026
Details
World map quality-of-life — reveals the map, shows dungeon icons and coordinates, lets you scale/reposition the map, removes the border, and stops zoom from resetting. Configurable via /ltm.
Leatrix Plus Download
Forever version 1.60.11-forever · Oct 2, 2026
Details
Modular UI quality-of-life addon — quest/repair automation, social and chat tweaks, minimap and tooltip improvements, frame management, and a few system optimizations. Enable only the pieces you want.
Prat 3.0 Download
Forever version 3.9.112 · Oct 2, 2026
Details
Chat upgrades: timestamps, class-colored names, message filters and copying text out of chat. One download covers every game version, WoW Forever included.
Questie Download
Forever version v12.0.3+v1.0.4 · Sep 30, 2026
Details
Shows quest givers, objectives and turn-ins on your map and minimap, with quest tooltips on mobs and items. One download covers every Classic flavor and WoW Forever. Forever's brand-new quests may be missing from its database while the data catches up.
ThreatMaster Download
Forever version 0.4.0 · Sep 28, 2026
Details
A threat meter styled after the game's built-in Damage Meter. Shows threat standing against the current target and warns both DPS and tanks when aggro is about to switch. Slash command conflict: claims /tm for itself, which is also the shorthand for WoW's built-in /targetmarker command — if you have a raid-mark macro using /tm (like our own mark macros used to), it'll silently call ThreatMaster instead of marking your target. Fixed on our macros page by switching to the full /targetmarker name — do the same for any of your own macros using /tm.
TomTom Download
Forever version v4.3.11-release · Sep 23, 2026
Details
Set waypoints by command or by clicking the map, then follow a floating arrow to get there. Shows your coordinates and can bulk-import waypoint lists for questing.
