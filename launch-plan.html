The rules that shape the route

Quests carry XP

Same XP curve as Classic, but dungeon mob kills pay less. Quests are the engine.

First clear, then leave

The first clear of each dungeon pays raised quest XP. Collect every quest for it first, clear once, move on.

Dodge the wave

Crowds move through zones in a wave. Skip Westfall on day one, go where fewer people start, and revisit busy zones after the wave has passed.

No scaling, no flying

Zones are as dangerous as their level. New boats shorten travel: Stormwind → Auberdine and Menethil → Southshore.
