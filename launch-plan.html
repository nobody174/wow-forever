Key differences at a glance

From Ironforge on, the zones and dungeons are the same for every plan. What changes is the start, money, travel and how the group plays.

The rules that shape the route

Quests carry XP

Same XP curve as Classic, but dungeon mob kills pay less. Quests are the engine.

First clear, then leave

The first clear of each dungeon pays raised quest XP. Collect every quest for it first, clear once, move on.

Dodge the wave

Crowds move through zones in a wave. Skip Westfall on day one, go where fewer people start, and revisit busy zones after the wave has passed.

No scaling, no flying

Zones are as dangerous as their level. New boats shorten travel: Stormwind → Auberdine and Menethil → Southshore.

Group play

Legacy points

Account-wide perks. Easy to misread: nobody starts with 16 points.

How it works

  • Every account starts at 0. Each completed Legacy challenge gives 1 point.
  • Points are shared by every character on the Battle.net account, so each of us earns our own.
  • Each character spends the points separately and can pick different perks.
  • 16 is a cap: the most one character can spend at launch.

Fastest points for us

  • First time a class reaches 25, 45 and 60: 3 points, earned just by leveling.
  • Spelunker challenges: clear a set of dungeons in each level bracket (exact lists unconfirmed).
  • Crafting professions at 150 / 225 / 300. Skinning and Cooking don't count.
  • Realistic total at our first 60: about 5–6 points. 16 needs alts, crafting or PvP.

Column gates

  • Column 1: open from the first point.
  • Column 2: needs 5 points spent in that tree.
  • Column 3: needs 10 points in that tree, plus a rank in the perk it's connected to. Its perk is point 11 at the earliest.

Well Rested & Talented, simply

  • Well Rested (col 1, 5 ranks): rested XP builds 4% faster and the rested pool holds 4% more, per rank. Rested XP only doubles kill XP, not quest XP. Best on alts left logged out for days.
  • Talented (col 2, 5 ranks, needs a rank in Well Rested): first talent point one level earlier per rank, level 9 → level 5 at 5/5. The total stays 51. Only helps a new character, so it's an alt perk.

Skyborne group path everyone

  • Points 1–5: Thrill of Adventure 5/5. It also saves food and water we'd otherwise buy.
  • Then stay in Adventure: Field Guide first (Frequent Flier's connector), then High Alert or Field Medicine.
  • Point 11: Frequent Flier (50% cheaper flights, 20% faster). The last 5 go to Resourcefulness column 1: Gourmand 3/3 plus The Quick and the Dead 2/2.

Paladin · Hunter · Shaman path trio

  • Same order for all three. Points 1–5: Thrill of Adventure 5/5. Its mana return matters most for the Paladin and Shaman.
  • Then stay in Adventure: Field Guide first (Frequent Flier's connector), then High Alert or Field Medicine.
  • Point 11: Frequent Flier. Nobody in this group teleports, so cheaper, faster flights help everyone.
