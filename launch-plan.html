The rules that shape the route

Quests carry XP

Same XP curve as Classic, but dungeon mob kills pay less. Quests are the engine.

First clear, then leave

The first clear of each dungeon pays raised quest XP. Collect every quest for it first, clear once, move on.

Dodge the wave

Crowds move through zones in a wave. Skip Westfall on day one, go where fewer people start, and revisit busy zones after the wave has passed.

No scaling, no flying

Zones are as dangerous as their level. New boats shorten travel: Stormwind → Auberdine and Menethil → Southshore.

Duo play

Mage duo tactics

  • Frost for leveling: Nova, then Frostbolt from range.
  • Alternate Frost Nova so one is always ready.
  • Decide up front who Polymorphs and who Counterspells.
  • One conjures food/water for both; the other keeps killing.
  • Keep Arcane Intellect on each other.

Skyborne racials

  • Read Ley Line: +100% health and mana regen. Stagger it so we're never both drinking.
  • Walk on Air: glide off cliffs for shortcuts.
  • Wind Blessed: +1% haste, passive.

Crowded camps

  • Invite anyone fighting us for the same mobs — kill credit is shared in a group.
  • Kill quests go fast as a duo; collection quests can be slower. Do those where mobs are dense.
  • Always carry a backup quest nearby so a packed camp never means waiting.
  • Deliveries and object pickups first when a camp is contested.

Professions & gold

  • Skinning for both; whoever didn't loot skins.
  • Cooking on at least one of us: campfire buffs + XP food.
  • Keep linen/wool to sell (or bandages later).
  • Spend in order: training → bags → mount fund.

XP extras

  • Start every session at a campfire (sit ~1 min, 1-hour buffs).
  • Keep a food buff up; food is being tested with a kill-XP bonus.
  • Log out in inns or cities for rested XP.
  • Don't let quests go gray — drop escorts and low-drop-rate quests.

The third player

A druid is the best fit: bear tank for first clears while two mages AoE, healer on elite quests, cat or bear for questing. Skyborne druids also get their own forms.

Test in beta

Beta runs until Oct 21 (cap 20, later 30) — exactly the 12–30 stretch. Split the list between us.
