WoW Forever macros

Short damage and mouseover-first support macros. Pick a class, filter by spec, and copy straight into the macro window. Prefer plain text? Read the full list as markdown.

Select ▾
Tick the macros you want in the game, or select a whole group with its checkbox.

Now type /r2f in the game and click Import.

How to import (3 steps)
  1. Install the Road to Forever addon: Download the newest RoadToForever-<version>.zip from GitHub Releases and unzip it into your game's Interface\AddOns\ folder.
  2. Pick macros here and click Copy import string.
  3. In the game, type /r2f, click Import, paste with Ctrl+V, click Import.
